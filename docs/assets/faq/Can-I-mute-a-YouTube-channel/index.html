Can I mute a YouTube channel?

Yes, you can mute a YouTube channel using the Channel Groups and Rating extension. Here’s how this extension allows you to manage the audio and visibility of videos from specific channels: How to Mute a YouTube Channel with the Extension Create a Group for the Channel: You can create a group specifically for the channels you want to mute. This allows you to manage multiple channels at once. Disable the Sound Toggle: In the group settings, you can turn off the Sound toggle. This will mute all videos from the channels added to this group, effectively removing the audio when you watch videos from these channels. Enable the Blur Thumbnail Option: You can also enable the Blur thumbnail option for the group. This will blur the thumbnails of the videos from these channels, making them less distracting in your feed. Additional Useful Features View Channel Names: Even if the video thumbnails are blurred, the channel names will still be clearly visible. This allows you to identify the source of the content without being distracted by the video itself. Reveal Thumbnail on Hover: When you hover over the blurred thumbnail, the hidden image returns to its original view. This lets you quickly assess whether the video is of interest without fully engaging with it. By using these features, you can effectively manage your YouTube experience, focusing on content that aligns with your interests while muting channels that do not meet your needs.

How do I export full YouTube time statistics, how long I watched data for each day and how long I watched a specific video until what point?

The Channel Groups and Rating extension for YouTube allows users to export detailed viewing statistics without needing to log into Google or enable viewing history on YouTube. All data is stored locally, and synchronization with Google Drive is available if desired. Key Features for Exporting Viewing Statistics Analytics Overview: The extension provides comprehensive insights into your viewing habits, including: Time Spent: Detailed breakdown of time spent watching videos, organized by days of the week and time of day. Video Details: Information about video languages, genres, and the percentage of watched segments. Channel Activity: Displays activity for each channel, including time spent, percentage of views, last viewed moment, and the number of videos watched. Playlist Creation: Allows you to create playlists from previously watched videos, with the option to select a date range for the last viewing, helping you identify videos you haven't finished. Export Options: The extension enables users to export all gathered analytics data directly to Excel. This feature is essential for users who want to analyze their viewing statistics in detail or keep a record of their YouTube activity. Local Data Storage and Google Drive Sync: The extension functions without requiring a Google account, meaning all viewing statistics are stored locally on your device. If you choose to log into Google, you can securely save the extension data on your Google Drive for synchronization across devices. No History Required: The extension does not require the activation of YouTube's viewing history, ensuring user privacy while still providing comprehensive analytics. By utilizing the Channel Groups and Rating extension, you can efficiently manage and export your YouTube viewing statistics without the need for account logins or history tracking, while benefiting from detailed analytics and the option for Google Drive synchronization.
