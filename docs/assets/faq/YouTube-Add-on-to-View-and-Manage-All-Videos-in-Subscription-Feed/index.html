YouTube Add-on to View and Manage All Videos in Subscription Feed

Channel groups and rating: A Powerful YouTube Subscription Manager
The Channel groups and rating extension for YouTube is a comprehensive tool that allows you to effectively manage your YouTube subscriptions and viewing experience. Here are some key features that address the question:

Grouping and Filtering Channels

  • You can create groups for your YouTube channels, enabling you to organize them into categories.
  • The extension provides filters to view videos based on type (watched, premieres, scheduled, shorts, etc.), rating, and YouTube subscription or channel groups.

Video Blocking and Filters

  • You can block videos based on keywords in the title, absence of keywords, keywords in the channel name or full group name.
  • The extension saves video filters, allowing you to choose the desired filter.
  • It creates playlists based on the filtering results.

Analytics

  • The extension displays information about time spent watching videos, broken down by days of the week and time of day.
  • It shows activity information for each channel, including time spent, percentage of views, last viewed moment, and number of videos.
  • You can create a playlist with videos from each channel that you have previously watched, with the option to select a date range.

Other Features

  • The extension allows you to rotate videos, change playback speed, replace sound and video for a specified group.
  • It skips a specified number of seconds in a video for a selected channel.
  • Automatically syncs with Google Drive if you sign in using the extension icon.

With the Channel groups and rating extension, you can effectively manage and view all the videos in your YouTube subscription feed, making your viewing experience more efficient and personalized.
