YouTube Removed the Trending Tab – Why and How to Replace It

Yes, they removed another feature again, just like they removed collections and dislikes.

Why is this happening?

I don't know how you can remove something without offering an analogy in return. If a developer changes something, you expect them to at least keep the old features. Even if they want to simplify their interface by removing the extra text and link to the trending page, they could hide the text out of sight. There is always such an option.

Could the reason for the removal be the cost of computing to create trends? As we remember, they only contained videos sorted by the number of views for a limited time. AI is not needed for this, the cost of maintenance is almost zero, considering that the necessary statistics are already used in other functions.

The main reason is because they can. Large companies often make mistakes due to management problems and poor feedback from users, but if a large company still has monopoly power, then everything becomes much worse.

What can be done.

Let's take a closer look at the trending function. Is it true that the trending function is global trends? No, it is not, you could easily verify this by changing the country in your settings, for different countries we received different trends. YouTube trends included videos with a large number of views in a short time, but these videos were related specifically to you.

How exactly these videos related to you you could not fully control. But you can create your own trends through subscriptions.

The Channel groups and rating extension has the ability to filter by the number of views in your subscriptions. If you subscribe to channels that are important to you, you can use this filter to get results with a large number of views.
