Can I Mute a YouTube Channel Using Channel Groups and Rating Extension?

Yes, you can mute a YouTube channel using the Channel groups and rating extension. Here’s how this extension allows you to manage the audio and visibility of videos from specific channels: How to Mute a YouTube Channel with the Extension Create a Group for the Channel: You can create a group specifically for the channels you want to mute. This allows you to manage multiple channels at once. Disable the Sound Toggle: In the group settings, you can turn off the Sound toggle. This will mute all videos from the channels added to this group, effectively removing the audio when you watch videos from these channels. Enable the Blur Thumbnail Option: You can also enable the Blur thumbnail option for the group. This will blur the thumbnails of the videos from these channels, making them less distracting in your feed. Additional Useful Features View Channel Names: Even if the video thumbnails are blurred, the channel names will still be clearly visible. This allows you to identify the source of the content without being distracted by the video itself. Reveal Thumbnail on Hover: When you hover over the blurred thumbnail, the hidden image returns to its original view. This lets you quickly assess whether the video is of interest without fully engaging with it. By using these features, you can effectively manage your YouTube experience, focusing on content that aligns with your interests while muting channels that do not meet your needs.
