Why YouTube removed the Trending tab and alternatives with extension

Yes, they've gone and removed another feature, just like they did with collections and dislikes.

Why does this keep happening?

I don't understand how you can just remove something without offering a replacement. When a developer makes a change, you'd expect them to at least keep the old functionality. Even if they want to simplify the interface by removing text and links to the trending page, they could have just hidden it out of sight. That's always an option.

Could the reason for this removal be the computational cost of generating trends? As we remember, the trending list was just videos sorted by view count over a short period. You don't need AI for that&mdash;the maintenance cost is practically zero, especially since the necessary statistics are already being used for other features.

The main reason, I think, is simply because they can. Large companies often make mistakes due to management issues and poor user feedback. But when a large company has a near-monopoly, everything becomes much worse.

So, what can we do?

Let's take a closer look at the trending feature itself. Was it really about global trends? Not exactly. If you changed your country settings, you'd see that trends varied by region. YouTube's trends showcased videos that were getting a lot of views quickly, but these videos were also somewhat personalized to your interests.

How exactly these videos were selected for you wasn't something you could fully control. However, you can create your own personal trending feed through your subscriptions.

Extensions like Channel groups and rating allow you to filter your subscriptions by view count. If you subscribe to channels that matter to you, you can use this filter to see which of their videos are getting the most traction, effectively creating your own trending list.
