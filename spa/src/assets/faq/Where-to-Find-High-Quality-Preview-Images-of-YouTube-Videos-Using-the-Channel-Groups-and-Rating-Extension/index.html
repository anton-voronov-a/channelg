Where to Find High-Quality Preview Images of YouTube Videos Using the Channel Groups and Rating Extension

The Channel groups and rating extension enables you to view a list of videos from your YouTube subscriptions with high-quality preview images.

To find these images, open the Videos dialog located in the extension's panel on your YouTube subscriptions page.

Once the list appears, you can right-click on any preview image and choose to save or use it according to your needs.

This feature simplifies accessing high-resolution thumbnails directly through the extension, enhancing your YouTube browsing experience.

How to Mark YouTube Channels as Spam or Low-Quality Using the Channel Groups and Rating Extension

To mark YouTube channels as bad, you need to use the Channel groups and rating extension. Just like an operating system uses a trash bin for garbage, this extension lets you create a special YouTube folder for spam or junk channels.

With this extension, you can group channels that you consider low-quality or spam by placing them into this dedicated folder. This organization helps you manage unwanted content effectively.

The name of this YouTube folder is displayed on the video viewing page, so by grouping channels this way, you can immediately understand the quality rating of the video you are watching.

Using the Channel groups and rating extension enhances your YouTube experience by allowing you to filter and rate channels, making it easier to avoid bad content and focus on quality videos.
